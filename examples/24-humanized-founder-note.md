# The Humanized Founder Note

Turn a stiff AI-generated announcement into a natural founder note and then adapt it into a LinkedIn draft.

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Humanize the announcement

```
Read about/me.md and strategy/brand.md, then humanize this announcement so it sounds like a founder wrote it, not a press release.

Keep the facts intact:
- We are launching a private beta next Tuesday
- The product helps B2B teams turn sales calls into usable account notes
- We have 12 design partners
- The ask is for 20 more beta users

Draft:
"We are excited to announce the launch of our groundbreaking private beta, a pivotal milestone in our journey to transform how B2B teams capture and operationalize customer intelligence. This innovative solution leverages AI to enhance sales workflows, streamline account documentation, and unlock actionable insights at scale. As we continue to build in close collaboration with our valued design partners, we invite forward-thinking teams to join us in shaping the future of revenue operations."
```

### Step 2 — Adapt it for LinkedIn

```
Turn the final founder note into a LinkedIn post.

Use the same voice, keep it under 180 words, avoid hype, and end with a direct ask for beta users.
```

## Output

- `content/announcements/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD_private-beta-founder-note.md`
- `content/linkedin/drafts/YYYY-MM-DD_private-beta-linkedin-post.md`
